Shaping Future Doctors & Engineers

Sri Lanka's most trusted name for A/L Biology & Combined Mathematics excellence. We don't just teach — we mentor the next generation of innovators, healers, and problem-solvers.

Our Mission

At Flux Academic, we believe every student has the potential to excel when given the right guidance, resources, and environment. Our mission is to demystify the most challenging A/L subjects and make them accessible, engaging, and achievable.

Since 2012, we've helped over 5,000 students transition from anxious school-leavers into confident university undergraduates — many now practicing as doctors, engineers, and researchers across the globe.

From the first lesson to the final revision, we're obsessed with the small details that turn an average result into an exceptional one.

What Drives Us

  • Purpose first Every lesson is aligned with the A/L syllabus and the real exam — no filler, no fluff.
  • Small, focused batches We cap class sizes so every student gets individual attention and direct access to teachers.
  • Proven results A consistent 94%+ pass rate with top-100 island ranks every single year.
  • Long-term mentorship We stay with our students from the first lesson through to university admission.
